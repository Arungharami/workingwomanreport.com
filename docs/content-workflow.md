# Publishing This Week's Story

1. Create one canonical weekly record in `content/weekly`.
2. Add the corresponding platform package in `content/social`.
3. Add verified sources, captions, transcript, image rights notes, and SEO.
4. Open `/studio` locally with `ENABLE_STUDIO=true`.
5. Review Article, Video Metadata, Social Package, SEO, Accessibility, and the
   Publishing Checklist.
6. Confirm Allison approval before publishing the website, video, or social copy.

## Weekly Operating Model

Monday: topic selection and research.
Tuesday: reporting, interview, and material collection.
Wednesday: article and TV/video preparation.
Thursday: edit, Allison approval, SEO, and social package.
Friday: publish flagship website/video package.
Weekend: short-form clips, social engagement, and analytics.

The schedule is a template. Adjust timing around Allison's actual TV schedule.
