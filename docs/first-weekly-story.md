# First Weekly Story Onboarding

This is the handoff checklist for Allison's first production-ready weekly report.

## What We Need From Allison

- Weekly topic
- Working headline
- TV station or program information, if applicable
- Air date
- Interviewee names and titles
- Interview notes
- Transcript
- Primary sources
- Photos with rights confirmation
- Video files or YouTube ID
- Approved quotes
- Allison commentary
- Call to action
- Final approval

## Step 1 - Add Materials

Place approved images, thumbnails, captions, transcripts, and production notes in
the weekly media folder. Add source links and rights notes to the weekly story
record.

## Step 2 - Generate Draft

Run `npm run story:new -- "Topic Name"` to create blank weekly and social files.
The command creates structure only. It does not invent article copy.

## Step 3 - Verify Facts

Check names, numbers, dates, quotes, statistics, source links, image rights,
video rights, captions, and alt text.

## Step 4 - Allison Review

Open Studio locally and confirm that Allison has reviewed the master story,
video information, SEO, and platform copy.

## Step 5 - Prepare Social Package

Adapt the story into YouTube metadata, vertical-video concepts, Instagram,
Facebook, TikTok, X, and newsletter copy. Do not duplicate the same copy blindly.

## Step 6 - Publish Website

Set the weekly story status to `published` only after approval and verification.

## Step 7 - Publish Video

Publish or schedule YouTube/video using the approved metadata and captions.

## Step 8 - Publish Social

Use the prepared copy manually or through future official adapters after
credentials are configured.

## Step 9 - Record Analytics

After publication, record website views, video views, reach, engagement, clicks,
followers added, best platform, best content, notes, and next-week recommendation.
